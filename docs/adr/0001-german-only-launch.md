# Launch in German only for v1

The app targets the German market first. All UI copy, App Store listing, and onboarding are in German only. English localisation is deferred to v2.

The German market was chosen because the founder is based in Germany and understands the local grocery landscape (REWE, EDEKA, Lidl). Launching in one language reduces copywriting and QA surface area for v1 and allows ranking in the German App Store / Google Play store before competing globally. Every competitor requires a cloud account; "Keine Cloud, keine Registrierung" (no cloud, no registration) is a stronger privacy signal in Germany than almost anywhere else in Europe.

**Considered options**: English-only (rejected — no personal distribution channel there), English + German simultaneously (rejected — doubles localisation work before product is validated).
